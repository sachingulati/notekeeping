## Question
What standing choices have I made that are not any one project's?

## Admission
A choice among alternatives that applies across your work and that someone - including you - will ask
about later.

## Exclusion
Specific to one project -> that project's decisions.md. A preference with no alternative weighed ->
it is not a decision.

## Entry format
A ledger, exactly as at project scope. Entries are never edited; a changed decision is a new dated
entry marking the old one superseded. `Would reopen if:` is required.
